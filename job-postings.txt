DOG WALKER
Part-time | Location-based rotation | Austin, TX
============================================================

ABOUT THE ROLE

We are looking for reliable, attentive dog walkers who understand that great dog care is about more than exercise. You will provide safe, thoughtful walks and visits for dogs assigned to your service area while working as part of a coordinated local team.

Walkers are assigned to a consistent location-based rotation. A project manager handles the schedule, owner communication, and handoffs so you can focus on delivering excellent care to each dog.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

- Safely walk and care for dogs according to their individual needs
- Arrive on time and follow scheduled handoff procedures
- Pay attention to dog behavior, energy levels, and safety concerns
- Provide clear visit updates and photos to the project manager
- Follow feeding, medication, access, and special-care instructions
- Return each dog safely to the proper owner or approved handoff point
- Communicate promptly about schedule changes, incidents, or concerns
- Maintain a clean, respectful presence in clients' homes and neighborhoods

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

- Demonstrated experience handling dogs, professionally or personally
- Strong awareness of dog body language and safe walking practices
- Dependability, punctuality, and excellent follow-through
- Comfort handling dogs of different breeds, sizes, ages, and energy levels
- Ability to communicate clearly by email and mobile messaging
- Reliable transportation to your assigned service area
- Willingness to complete practical dog-care onboarding and safety training
- A calm, patient, and genuinely caring approach to animals

LOCAL ELIGIBILITY

Applicants must live in or near their assigned service area and have a current government-issued state ID with a U.S. address.

WHAT WE OFFER

- Part-time work organized around a local service-area rotation
- Clear scheduling and support from a dedicated project manager
- The opportunity to build trusted relationships with dogs and owners
- A team environment built around safety, consistency, and accountability

HOW TO APPLY

Email hello@pawsfectwalks.com with the subject line "Dog Walker Application" and include:

- Your name and the Austin area where you live
- A short summary of your dog-handling experience
- Your general availability
- Your transportation situation
- Two references, if available

PROJECT MANAGER
Operations | Small team lead | Austin, TX
============================================================

ABOUT THE ROLE

Pawsfect Walks is looking for a highly organized Project Manager to run the day-to-day coordination of a small team of dog walkers. This person will be the familiar, accountable face owners communicate with — not the walkers themselves.

The Project Manager makes sure every dog is matched with the right walker, every handoff is covered, and every owner receives timely, professional communication. This is an operations role for someone who enjoys creating order, solving problems early, and leading a small team with care.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

- Coordinate daily schedules and assign dogs to the appropriate walkers
- Build and maintain location-based walker rotations
- Match each dog with a walker based on temperament, needs, experience, and availability
- Confirm pickups, handoffs, walks, and returns are covered
- Serve as the primary point of contact for dog owners
- Send clear updates and respond professionally to owner questions
- Maintain accurate dog profiles, care instructions, access details, and schedules
- Onboard, support, and hold walkers accountable to company standards
- Handle schedule changes, call-outs, delays, and coverage gaps
- Escalate safety or health concerns quickly and appropriately
- Review visit updates and make sure owners receive consistent service
- Improve processes as the team and client base grow

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

- Exceptional organization and attention to detail
- Experience coordinating people, schedules, logistics, or client operations
- Ability to manage a small team without losing sight of individual details
- Strong written communication and professional judgment
- Confidence communicating with pet owners and resolving problems calmly
- Ability to prioritize multiple time-sensitive tasks
- Comfort using calendars, email, spreadsheets, and scheduling tools
- Reliable transportation in the Austin area
- Dog-care experience is strongly preferred
- Willingness to complete practical dog-care onboarding and safety training

LOCAL ELIGIBILITY

Applicants must live in or near Austin and have a current government-issued state ID with a U.S. address.

SUCCESS IN THIS ROLE LOOKS LIKE

- Every dog has the right walker and every visit has clear ownership
- Owners know who to contact and receive timely, useful updates
- Walkers understand their schedules, responsibilities, and service areas
- Handoffs are calm, safe, and consistent
- Problems are identified and solved before they affect the client experience

WHAT WE OFFER

- The opportunity to shape the operating system of a growing local dog-care business
- A leadership role with direct ownership of team coordination and client experience
- A small, collaborative team environment
- Room to grow as Pawsfect Walks expands its service areas
