**Status: owner approval and real walker capacity pending.**

## Resolve the current mismatch

Native `ServiceAreas.tsx` describes Central/Downtown, South, East and North Austin; `Contact.tsx` says Austin and surrounding areas. These are marketed zones, not evidence of coverage. ILL `portfolio/sites.json` describes central Austin evening/weekend requests. Do not publish either as confirmed capacity.

## Get it done

1. Jordan selects one initial zone and records its neighborhood/postal-code boundaries privately/publicly as appropriate. Treat every address near a boundary as requiring manual review; ZIP codes alone are not routing guarantees.
2. Collect each vetted walker's offered availability, maximum daily visits, acceptable dog needs, travel access and backup capacity privately. Do not derive business hours from Jordan's employment/sleep schedule or publish personal schedules.
3. Define booking windows in **America/Chicago**, the time a walker must be present, customer update timing and who monitors issues during every visit. No automatic acceptance.
4. Rehearse a route with synthetic/test addresses and realistic travel, parking, building access, dog handoff and reporting time. A 30-minute walk needs additional working time. Start with a proposed 15-minute buffer, then increase it from measured route evidence.
5. Name a primary and approved backup for each offered window. If no safe backup exists, cap capacity and explicitly handle cancellation rather than promise coverage.
6. Approve business hours and rate card together. Reconcile native service duration/rate, ILL preview estimator and invoices. Do not set new prices merely to make the templates match.
7. Update native copy only after approval. Broader zones remain waitlist-only; inquiry is not a booking.
