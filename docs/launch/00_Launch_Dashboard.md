## Proposed small pilot — requires approval

One service zone; 2–3 vetted walkers; 2–3 clients; individual household walks and simple agreed drop-ins. The native repository describes a manager coordinating local walkers, not Jordan personally fulfilling every booking. No routine pet transport, boarding, off-leash/group walks or medication administration in the proposed pilot. In-home sitting is an advertised native service but must remain unavailable until separately scoped and covered.

Contractor engagement is a prior intention, not a legally settled classification. Resolve the actual direction/control and compensation arrangement before assigning paid work. See the insurance and payment tasks. Do not grow staffing before confirming real demand and safe capacity.
