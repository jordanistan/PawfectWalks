# 06 — Cancellation and refund policy

**DRAFT proposal; not approved or published as binding terms.** Native Policies.tsx currently defers timing/fees to onboarding. Do not charge any proposed fee until terms are approved, disclosed and accepted before payment.

## Proposed decision table

| Event | Proposed treatment |
|---|---|
| Client cancels at least 24 hours before confirmed visit window begins | Full refund of that unused visit |
| Client cancels less than 24 hours before window begins | 50% cancellation charge, remaining 50% refunded; owner must approve or replace |
| Provider cancels / approved walker or backup unavailable | Full refund of unused service; no cancellation fee |
| Unsafe weather or safety concern prevents agreed care | Agreed safe alternative only with prior consent; otherwise full refund of unperformed service |
| Owner access failure despite documented contact attempts | Proposed late-cancellation treatment only if specifically disclosed; manager reviews emergency exceptions |
| Care already partly delivered or disputed | Review facts, document performed/unperformed portion, apply signed terms; no automatic denial |
| Duplicate/incorrect payment or service declined before acceptance | Correct/refund promptly; no booking created by payment alone |
| Genuine emergency / provider error | Manager reviews fairly; do not penalize provider-caused failure |

Use **America/Chicago** and the beginning of the confirmed arrival window; capture the cancellation timestamp. Refund to the original payment method where supported. A credit is optional only with customer agreement, never a forced substitute for a promised refund.

## Procedure

1. Record private visit/payment IDs, request time, confirmed window and reason. Notify dispatch immediately so no walker arrives unnecessarily.
2. Calculate result from the signed policy/version and actual service status; manager approves exceptions. Explain amount and calculation to the customer.
3. Proposed target: initiate an approved refund within two business days. Processor/bank delivery can take longer; verify current provider timing instead of promising an instant credit.
4. Initiate through the original processor, save refund reference/status, send confirmation and reconcile. If the original payment was outside the processor, arrange and verify an actual transfer; marking a record refunded is not proof money moved.
5. Handle failures/disputes with owner oversight. Do not expose receipts, card details or personal reasons in public tasks.
