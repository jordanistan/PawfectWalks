## Agreement fields and proposed clauses

1. **Parties.** Provider: [confirmed legal operator], trading as Pawsfect Walks, business contact [approved]. Customer: [private name/contact]. Service address and dog(s): [restricted care-plan reference]. Do not invent company formation or insurance status.
2. **Agreed service.** [approved individual walk/drop-in], duration [ ], dates/windows [ ], rate and any agreed extras [ ], designated walker and permitted backup [ ]. No routine transport, boarding, group/off-leash care, medication administration or overnight care unless explicitly added after readiness review. Customer acknowledges inquiries do not reserve visits.
3. **Acceptance and booking.** Service starts only after fit assessment, accurate care plan, agreement signature, verified payment and explicit written booking confirmation. Provider may decline before acceptance or pause unsafe care; any unused prepaid service follows the refund policy.
4. **Customer information and equipment.** Customer discloses known bite/escape history, triggers, mobility/health constraints, relevant vet instructions and emergency contacts; supplies suitable agreed equipment and lawful access. Changes must be communicated before the next visit. No promise that disclosure eliminates provider duties.
5. **Entry, keys and privacy.** Customer authorizes only the agreed access and assigned persons. Provider limits access, uses separate restricted code/key custody and returns/revokes it at termination. No access secrets in ordinary booking messages or public systems. Record retention and incident notice procedure: [reviewed private schedule].
6. **Weather and safety.** Provider may stop, shorten or cancel unsafe activity. An alternate service requires prior customer consent and an agreed price; otherwise refund unused service. No fee for provider cancellation or inability to safely fulfill the promised scope.
7. **Veterinary emergency.** Customer authorizes [approved emergency actions], regular/emergency veterinary contact [ ], alternate [ ], spending limit [ ], payer [ ], and safe transport arrangements [ ]. Reviewed procedure if contacts cannot be reached: [ ]. Do not use this draft to authorize unlimited spending or unreviewed transport.
8. **Payment and records.** Approved processor/method [ ], invoice timing [ ], prepayment scope [ ], receipt [ ], dispute/support contact [ ]. Cards stay with the processor. Provider verifies actual payment, not a screenshot or manually recorded status alone. Worker pay arrangements are separate from the client agreement.
9. **Cancellations/refunds.** Attach the approved version of [06_Cancellation_and_Refunds](06_Cancellation_and_Refunds.md), including timezone, notice threshold, company cancellation, safety/weather exceptions, access failure, refund initiation and original-method handling. Customer sees it before paying; no retroactive fees.
10. **Updates and photos.** Factual arrival/completion update by [agreed channel/time]. Service-update photo consent [ ]. Optional marketing consent [separate yes/no]; refusal does not prevent ordinary service. No publication of address/access information or live location.
11. **Substitution and termination.** Approved backup may fulfill only after customer notification and equivalent readiness. No arbitrary subcontracting beyond agreed terms/insurance. Either party may end future care under [approved notice]; outstanding balances/refunds and keys/access are reconciled.
12. **Complaints and legal review.** Customer reports concerns to [business contact]. Record investigation and response; do not replace rights with an unchecked waiver/arbitration clause. Counsel determines applicable law, notices, dispute language and required disclosures.
